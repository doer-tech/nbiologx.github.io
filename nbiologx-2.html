Our mission is simple: ensure that no Nepali patient is denied access to essential plasma-derived medicines because of supply shortages, import dependency, or affordability barriers.

NBiologx is a Nepal-based biotechnology company established to develop the country's first integrated plasma recovery, plasma fractionation, and plasma-derived medicinal products (PDMPs) ecosystem.

By leveraging Nepal's existing blood collection infrastructure and aligning with World Health Organization (WHO) recommendations, NBiologx aims to establish a nationally coordinated plasma utilization program — converting surplus plasma into high-value therapeutics including Human Albumin, Intravenous Immunoglobulin (IVIG), and Coagulation Factors.

Every year, Nepal imports critical plasma-derived medicines for intensive care, liver disease, hemophilia, immunodeficiency disorders, neurological diseases, and trauma care — while significant volumes of recoverable plasma remain underutilized in the national blood system.

Development Strategy

A Phased, Low-Risk Pathway to Self-Sufficiency

Rather than an immediate high-capital industrial facility, NBiologx follows internationally recognised phased development principles — reducing risk while accelerating impact.

01
Phase I
National Plasma Recovery Program
Establish fractionation-grade plasma collection, quality systems, donor traceability, and international contract fractionation partnerships.
→ Immediate production access without large-scale industrial infrastructure
02
Phase II
Domestic PDMP Manufacturing
Develop albumin production, fill-finish facilities, quality control laboratories and national biologics manufacturing capability.
→ Import substitution and improved medicine accessibility
03
Phase III
Advanced Plasma Processing
Expand capabilities for Intravenous Immunoglobulin, hyperimmune globulins and specialty plasma proteins.
→ Enhanced self-sufficiency and regional competitiveness
04
Phase IV
National Fractionation Facility
Establish Nepal's first full-scale plasma fractionation platform producing a comprehensive portfolio of PDMPs.
→ From Nepal to the World: a regional manufacturing hub
