About NBiologx

Ending Nepal's Dependence
on Imported Biologics

NBiologx is a Nepal-based biotechnology company established to develop the country's first integrated plasma recovery, fractionation, and plasma-derived medicinal products (PDMPs) ecosystem.

Our mission: ensure no Nepali patient is denied access to essential plasma-derived medicines because of supply shortages, import dependency, or affordability barriers.

By leveraging Nepal's existing blood collection infrastructure and aligning with World Health Organization (WHO) recommendations, NBiologx establishes a nationally coordinated plasma utilization program that converts surplus plasma into high-value therapeutic products — and positions Nepal as a future regional biologics hub.

The Opportunity

Strengthening
National Health Security

The COVID-19 pandemic exposed the fragility of global pharmaceutical supply chains. Countries without domestic plasma programs remain exposed to shortages, import disruptions, and rising costs.

01
Supply Chain Vulnerability

Nepal depends entirely on imports for critical biologics. Global shortages directly translate to patient care gaps and price spikes across Nepali hospitals.

02
Underutilised Domestic Plasma

Significant volumes of recoverable plasma from Nepal's national blood system remain discarded — a wasted national resource with immense therapeutic value.

03
Foreign Exchange Pressure

Import dependence places ongoing foreign exchange burden on Nepal's healthcare system, limiting access and increasing the true cost of care.

04
WHO-Endorsed Pathway

WHO recommends LMICs develop domestic plasma programs using a stepwise approach, with contract fractionation as the practical entry point.

Low-Risk, Stepwise Model
